Running late? Say so

Install

Up and running in four steps.

AgriTrade+ is installed from this page as an APK file, not from the Play Store yet.

  1. Download the app

    Tap Download for Android. The file, AgriTrade.apk, saves to your phone's Downloads.

  2. Open the file

    Tap the finished download in your notifications, or find it in the Files app under Downloads.

  3. Allow the install

    If your phone asks, turn on Allow from this source for your browser or Files app, go back, and tap Install.

  4. Sign up

    Open AgriTrade+, choose Buyer or Farmer, and fill in your details, or continue with Google.

On a computer?

Scan this with your Android phone's camera to download the app there.

What you need

  • An Android phone with Android 7.0 or newer. iPhone isn't available yet.
  • An internet connection, and an email address to sign up.
  • Location, if you allow it, to find your barangay and show distances. You can also pick your barangay by hand.
  • Farmers: your RSBSA number, for verification.

Questions

Good to know.

Is it free?

Yes. Downloading and using AgriTrade+ costs nothing. Buyers and farmers agree on the order in the app; payment is settled between them on pick-up or delivery.

Is it safe to install an app from an APK file?

This APK comes straight from AgriTrade+'s own website. Android asks you to allow installs from your browser because the app isn't on the Play Store yet. Only download AgriTrade+ from this page.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. AgriTrade+ is available for Android phones (Android 7.0 or newer).

Who can sell on AgriTrade+?

Farmers from Laurel, Batangas. Sign up as a Farmer with your RSBSA number and barangay. The AgriTrade+ team reviews every farmer account; once it's approved, log in again to start posting products.

Who decides the price of my product?

You do. When you post, AgriTrade+ shows a suggested price range from the current market as a guide, but you can set any price. Buyers decide whether to buy at it.

What can I sell?

Products from the supported list, under Grains, Root Crops, Vegetables, Spices, Fruits and Livestock. Livestock is sold live.

How do I accept or reject an order?

Open Orders, tap Details on the order, then Accept or Reject at the bottom. The quantity comes off your stock only when you accept; rejecting leaves your stock as it is.

What if I can't deliver on the agreed date?

Open the confirmed order and tap Report Delay. Choose the reason and propose a new date; the buyer can accept it or cancel the order.

How do I place an order?

Open a product and tap Order Now. Choose the quantity, pick-up or delivery, and the date you need it, then place your order. The farmer confirms it.

Why is delivery not available to my location?

Each farmer chooses the areas they deliver to. Message the farmer to ask them to add your area, or choose pick-up instead.

I already received my order. What do I do?

Open the order and tap Order Received. It completes the order, issues your receipt and lets you review the product.

How can I contact support?

Email the AgriTrade+ team at agritrade.team@gmail.com, or send a support request from Help & Support in the app.
